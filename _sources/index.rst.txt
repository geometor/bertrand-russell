================
Bertrand Russell
================

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of :person:`Bertrand Russell <russell>` (1872–1970), the British philosopher and logician whose early treatises on the foundations of geometry and the principles of mathematics reshaped twentieth-century logic, philosophy of space, and mathematical rigor. Within the interconnected `GEOMETOR <https://geometor.com/books/>`_ semantic digital archive, these works are liberated from static print bounds—decomposed into addressable semantic units, calibrated vector diagrams, primary-source concordance provenance, and cross-corpus citations speaking one shared geometric language.
