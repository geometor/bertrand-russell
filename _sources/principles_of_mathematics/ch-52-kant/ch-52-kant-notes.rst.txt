The Three-Cornered Debate: Kant, Russell, and Hoenen
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Russell (§434) and :person:`Hoenen <hoenen>` (:cite:`*De Noetica Geometriae*, Caput I–II & IV <hoenen:1954>`) both reject Kant's subjective *a priori* form of sensibility, yet diverge sharply on what replaces it: Russell banishes intuition completely from pure mathematics into hypothetico-deductive logicism, whereas Hoenen rehabilitates *objective* Aristotelian-Thomistic intellective intuition abstracted from sensory phantasms (*resolutio ad sensum*) to ground the truth of first principles.
