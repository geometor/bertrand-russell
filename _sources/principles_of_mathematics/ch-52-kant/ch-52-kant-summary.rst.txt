Summary — Chapter LII: Kant's Theory of Space (§§ 432–436)
==========================================================

*Primary Text:* :ref:`Read Verbatim Treatise Track (pp. 454–461) <pom-ch-52-kant-en>` · *Commentary:* :ref:`Critical Notes <pom-ch-52-kant-notes>`

Executive Summary
-----------------

Concluding Part VI, Chapter LII (*Kant's Theory of Space*, §§432–436, pp. 454–461) delivers :person:`Russell <russell>`'s definitive logicist critique of :person:`Immanuel Kant <kant>`'s :cite:`*Transcendental Aesthetic* <kant:1787:krv>` and *First/Second Mathematical Antinomies*. Russell shows that Cantor's transfinite continuum dissolves Kant's antinomies (§433), that modern relational logic Deduces all pure geometry without spatial construction in intuition (§434), that incongruent counterparts (left and right hands) arise purely from three-dimensional ordinal sense (§435), and summarizes the complete reduction of pure geometry to the logic of serial relations (§436).

Key Arguments & Theses
----------------------

§§433–434: Refutation of both Transcendental Dialectic and Aesthetic
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Kant's antinomies rest on confusing infinite wholes with endless temporal synthesis; and :person:`Pieri <pieri>`, :person:`Peano <peano>`, and :person:`Hilbert <hilbert>` prove that rigorous geometric proofs never inspect spatial figures, using them merely as psychological mnemonics.

§435: Incongruent Counterparts Without Pure Intuition
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Kant's argument from right and left hands (1768/1783) needs no non-conceptual intuition, being fully captured by difference of sense among ordered triads of rays in three dimensions (cf. §404).

§436: Summary of Part VI
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Pure geometry studies logically defined classes of multi-dimensional series whose existence is guaranteed by real arithmetic; whether actual physical space is Euclidean, hyperbolic, or elliptic belongs wholly to empirical science.
