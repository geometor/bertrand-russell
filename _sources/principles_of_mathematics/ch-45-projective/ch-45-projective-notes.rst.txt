Historical & Cross-Corpus Commentary
------------------------------------

Cross-Link to GEOMETOR Pappus Corpus
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Russell's §§365–373 provide the modern logicist culmination of the projective tradition originating in :person:`Pappus of Alexandria <pappus>` (:cite:`*Collection* Book VII <pappus:collection:vii>`, modeled in ``geometor/pappus``), where cross-ratio invariance and the four-line harmonic fourth were first proved metrically before :person:`von Staudt <staudt>` purified them of length.

Refinement of 1897 EFG §§111–121
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In :ref:`1897 Ch. III Sec. A <efg-113>`, Russell had not yet discovered :person:`Pieri <pieri>`'s 1898 proof deriving cyclic order purely from harmonic conjugates without assuming open segments.
