Historical & Cross-Corpus Commentary
------------------------------------

Connection to Rational & Projective Trigonometry (Wildberger)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Russell's §407 observation that rational algebraic functions of cross-ratios characterize collinear order and quadric polarity prior to transcendental logarithms directly anticipates modern algebraic/projective metrical formulations (cf. N. J. Wildberger's *Divine Proportions* and *Universal Hyperbolic Geometry*).

Explicit Self-Correction of 1897 EFG §§37 & 42
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Compare :ref:`EFG §37 <efg-37>`, where Russell argued projective coordinates could convey no genuinely metrical truth; in §406–407 Russell explains why logicism dissolves that objection.
