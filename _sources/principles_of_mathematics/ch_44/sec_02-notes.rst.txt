Textual & Epistemological Commentary

Contrast with 1897 EFG §§18–19 & §94
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In :ref:`1897 Ch. II §94 <efg-94>`, Russell used orders of infinity (:math:`\infty^2`) informally against :person:`Lotze <lotze>`; by 1903 (§355), having mastered :person:`Cantor <cantor>`'s proof that :math:`(2^{\alpha_0})^2 = 2^{\alpha_0}`, he relocates dimensionality strictly in the ordinal structure of generating relations.
