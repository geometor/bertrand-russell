Crucial Contrast Between 1897 (§§146–154) and 1903 (PoM §§390–391)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Comparing :ref:`EFG §147 <efg-147>` with :ref:`PoM §390 <pom-390>` illuminates the entire arc of Russell's thought and :person:`Hoenen <hoenen>`'s critique: in 1897 Russell treated :term:`Free Mobility <free-mobility>` as an *a priori* condition of spatial comparison; by 1903, convinced by Cantor and Weierstrass that motion reduces to static occupation of different positions at different times, he declared superposition 'a tissue of nonsense' and replaced mobility with static congruence relations.
