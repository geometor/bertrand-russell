.. _glossary:

Technical Glossary
==================

.. rubric:: Projective, Descriptive, Metrical, Logicist, and Scholastic Terminology

This lexicon defines **27 technical concepts** central to Russell's 1897 and 1903 geometric treatises and their reception in Hoenen and Heath. Every inline ``:term:`` role links directly to its entry below.

----

.. _term-anharmonic-ratio:

anharmonic ratio (cross-ratio)
------------------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English / French (rapport anharmonique)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Projective invariant of four collinear points or concurrent rays*

The double ratio (AB/CB) / (AD/CD) preserved under perspective projection; in pure projective geometry, assigned numerically via Möbius nets and Dedekind continuity without appeal to metrical distance (EFG §§111–112; PoM §370).

.. _term-free-mobility:

axiom of Free Mobility (Congruence)
-----------------------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English
* **Concise Formulation:** *Invariance of spatial magnitudes under continuous change of position*

In EFG (§§146–154), the a priori metrical postulate that spatial magnitudes are independent of place, equivalent to constant Riemannian curvature and homogeneity of space.

.. _term-betweenness:

betweenness
-----------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Descriptive Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Triadic open-order relation of collinear points*

The primitive order relation axiomatized by Pasch, Peano, and Hilbert, analyzed by Russell in PoM Part IV and Ch. XLVI as generated by transitive asymmetrical serial relations along a line.

.. _term-descriptive-geometry:

descriptive geometry
--------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Geometry of open serial order and betweenness (Pasch–Peano)*

In Russell's 1903 classification (PoM Ch. XLVI, §§374–387), the study of spaces where two points determine an open serial segment (betweenness) rather than a closed projective line—applicable to Euclidean and hyperbolic spaces prior to introducing metrical congruence, and completed into projective space via ideal elements.

.. _term-form-of-externality:

form of externality
-------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (1897 Epistemology)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Relational condition of experiencing simultaneous substantival diversity*

Central philosophical concept of Russell's 1897 Essay (Chs. III–IV): any intuitive relational medium rendering experience of diverse interrelated things possible—from which Russell deduced projective geometry and free mobility as a priori before abandoning transcendental arguments in 1903.

.. _term-formal-implication:

formal implication
------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Logicism)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Universal conditional over variables: φ(x) implies ψ(x) for all x*

Russell's foundational relation in PoM (1903, §§1, 5–6): an implication holding for every value of one or more variables, by which pure mathematics asserts hypothetical deductions ('if S is a Euclidean space, then P holds of S') without categorical existential commitments.

.. _term-harmonic-range:

harmonic range
--------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Projective Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Four collinear points (A, B, C, D) in harmonic conjugation*

Four collinear points such that A and C are harmonic conjugates with respect to B and D: metrically having cross-ratio -1, and projectively defined without metrics by von Staudt's complete quadrilateral construction (EFG §113; PoM §365).

.. _term-ideal-elements:

ideal elements (points, lines, and planes at infinity)
------------------------------------------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Descriptive Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Logical classes (sheaves/pencils) completing a descriptive space into a projective space*

Following Pasch, an 'ideal point' is defined logically—without postulating fictitious entities—as an entire bundle (sheaf) of lines meeting pairwise in planes, allowing parallel or ultra-parallel lines to share an ideal intersection (PoM §§383–386).

.. _term-involution:

involution
----------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Projective Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Symmetric projective pairing of collinear points with double points*

A projective correspondence on a line that interchanges conjugate pairs; hyperbolic involutions possess two real double points harmonic with every conjugate pair, whereas elliptic involutions have none (PoM §366).

.. _term-logical-constant:

logical constant
----------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Logicism)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Indefinable or purely logical primitive notion*

In PoM §1 & §10, the sole non-variable constituents permitted in propositions of pure mathematics: implication, class-membership (ε), 'such that', relation, and truth.

.. _term-magnitude-of-divisibility:

magnitude of divisibility
-------------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (PoM Metrical Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Quantitative measure of a stretch, area, or volume as a property of serial relations*

Russell's relational interpretation of extensive spatial quantity (PoM §§162, 396–397): since all continuous stretches have the same Cantor cardinality 2^α₀, length belongs not to the point-set itself but to equivalence classes of generating serial relations.

.. _term-manifold:

manifold (Mannigfaltigkeit)
---------------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English / German
* **Concise Formulation:** *Continuous or discrete n-tuply extended system of determinations*

Riemann's general class-concept of multiply extended magnitudes (1854), critiqued in EFG Ch. II (§§60–65) for neglecting qualitative relational order and reformulated logically in PoM Ch. XLIV as a multi-dimensional series.

.. _term-materia-intelligibilis:

materia intelligibilis
----------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** Latin (Scholastic / Aristotelian)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Intelligible matter (pure continuous extension)*

Aristotle's and Aquinas's designation (ὕλη νοητή) for continuous extension abstracted from sensible qualities, invoked by Hoenen (Caput IV & VII) as the principle of individuation and congruence answering Russell's attack on Euclid I.4.

.. _term-measure-of-curvature:

measure of curvature (Krümmungsmass)
------------------------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English / German
* **Concise Formulation:** *Intrinsic Gaussian / Riemannian curvature constant k*

Intrinsic parameter characterizing homogeneous spaces admitting free mobility: k = 0 for Euclidean space, k < 0 for Lobachevskian (hyperbolic) space, and k > 0 for spherical and elliptic spaces (EFG §§20–23).

.. _term-metageometry:

Metageometry
------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (19th-c. Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *General / non-Euclidean geometry investigating axiom independence*

Term used throughout Russell's 1897 Essay for the synthetic (Gauss, Lobachevsky, Bolyai), metrical-differential (Riemann, Helmholtz, Beltrami), and projective (Cayley, Klein, Lie) generalizations of Euclidean geometry.

.. _term-metrical-geometry:

metrical geometry
-----------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Quantitative geometry of distance, congruence, angle, area, and volume*

The branch of geometry introducing quantitative equality (congruence) of stretches, distances, and angles, presupposing projective or descriptive order (EFG Ch. III Sec. B; PoM Ch. XLVII).

.. _term-motion-geometric:

motion (geometrical)
--------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (PoM Metrical Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Class of metric-preserving one-one point transformations*

In PoM §§390 & 395, Russell's static relational replacement for kinematic transport: a group of bijective point-correlations leaving all distances and metrical properties invariant (axiomatized directly as an indefinable by Pieri, 1899).

.. _term-moebius-net:

Möbius net
----------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Projective Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Countable dense harmonic net on a projective line or plane*

The denumerable progression of points generated from three initial collinear points (0, 1, ∞) by repeated harmonic quadrilateral constructions, establishing rational projective coordinates and projective order (PoM §368).

.. _term-point-spatial:

point (spatial element)
-----------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Foundations of Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Simple unextended term of spatial relations*

Treated in EFG (§§117, 189–204) as infected with a dialectical contradiction between unextended simplicity and spatiality, then vindicated in PoM (Ch. LI, §§423–431) as a logically consistent substantive term of multi-dimensional serial relations.

.. _term-duality:

principle of duality
--------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Projective Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Reciprocity of points and planes in 3-space (or points and lines in 2-space)*

The formal symmetry of projective axioms under uniform interchange of 'point' and 'plane' (leaving 'straight line' invariant in three dimensions), doubling every projective theorem (EFG §107; PoM §373).

.. _term-projective-geometry:

projective geometry
-------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Qualitative geometry of incidence, collineation, and harmonic separation*

The geometry of properties invariant under central projection and section, requiring neither betweenness on open lines nor quantitative distance; axiomatized qualitatively via incidence and von Staudt's quadrilateral construction (EFG Ch. III Sec. A; PoM Ch. XLV).

.. _term-propositional-function:

propositional function
----------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Logicism)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Open expression φ(x) yielding a proposition for each value of x*

An expression containing one or more real variables which becomes a determinate true or false proposition whenever definite terms are substituted for its variables.

.. _term-quadrilateral-construction:

quadrilateral construction
--------------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Projective Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Von Staudt's pure-incidence construction of the harmonic fourth point*

Given collinear points A, B, D, constructing the unique harmonic conjugate C of A with respect to B, D by projecting from an external point O and intersecting diagonals of a complete quadrilateral—uniqueness in a plane following from three-dimensional incidence via Desargues's theorem (EFG §113; PoM §365).

.. _term-separation-of-couples:

separation of couples
---------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Order Theory / Projective Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Tetradic cyclic order relation on a closed series (ab // cd)*

Vailati's five-axiom symmetrical transitive relation between point-pairs (a, c) and (b, d) on a closed projective or elliptic line where simple triadic betweenness fails because any two points bound two complementary segments (PoM §§194, 367, 398).

.. _term-stretch:

stretch
-------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (PoM Theory of Quantity)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Open or closed segment of points between two endpoints on a line*

The class of intermediate terms (or the generating serial relation restricted to them) between two collinear points A and B, contrasted by Russell with 'distance', which is an indivisible two-term relation (PoM §§169–170, 393–398).

.. _term-superposition:

superposition
-------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English (Classical Geometry)
* **Concise Formulation:** *Geometric proof of congruence by transporting one figure onto another*

Euclid's method in I.4, I.8, and C.N. 4; rejected by Russell in PoM §390 as logically circular because spatial points are immobile positions while moving material bodies presupposes an antecedent metrical definition of rigidity; rehabilitated epistemologically by Hoenen via extension as materia intelligibilis.

.. _term-the-absolute:

the Absolute (Grundgebild)
--------------------------

* **Tradition / Domain:** English / German
* **Concise Formulation:** *Invariant conic or quadric locus defining a Cayley–Klein projective metric*

Cayley and Klein's fixed second-degree locus in projective space relative to which distance and angle are logarithmic functions of cross-ratios: an imaginary quadric yields elliptic geometry, a real non-degenerate quadric yields hyperbolic geometry, and a degenerate imaginary conic in a plane yields Euclidean geometry (EFG §31; PoM §§407–409).
