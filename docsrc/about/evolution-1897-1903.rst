Philosophical Turn (1897 → 1903): Form of Externality vs. Logic of Relations
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Comparative Architecture of *An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry* (1897) and *The Principles of Mathematics* (1903)

Reading :cite:`*An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry* (1897) <russell:1897>` alongside Part VI of :cite:`*The Principles of Mathematics* (1903) <russell:1903>` reveals a dramatic six-year transformation across every foundational concept of geometry. In the :ref:`1903 Preface <pom-preface-en>` and footnotes (:ref:`PoM § 393 <pom-393>`, :ref:`§ 428 <pom-428>`), :person:`Bertrand Russell <russell>` explicitly repudiates the philosophical doctrines of his 1897 book wherever they conflict with his new logicist analysis.

Doctrinal Comparison Matrix
---------------------------

.. list-table:: Structural Shift Across Russell's Two Geometric Treatises
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 39 39

   * - Foundational Question
     - 1897 *Foundations of Geometry* (:ref:`Codex II <efg-index>`)
     - 1903 *Principles of Mathematics* (:ref:`Codex I <pom-index>`)
   * - **Nature of Pure Geometry**
     - Transcendental deduction of the *a priori* conditions of any possible :term:`form of externality <form-of-externality>` (:ref:`EFG §§ 3–8 <efg-3>`, :ref:`§§ 131–140 <efg-132>`).
     - Purely hypothetical :term:`formal implication <formal-implication>` (``p implies q``) defining classes of relational entities via :term:`logical constants <logical-constant>` alone (:ref:`PoM §§ 1–10 <pom-1>`, :ref:`§ 352 <pom-352>`, :ref:`§ 412 <pom-412>`).
   * - **Taxonomy of Geometries**
     - Twofold division: qualitative **Projective Geometry** (:ref:`Ch. IIIA <efg-ch-03a-projective-en>`) vs. quantitative **Metrical Geometry** (:ref:`Ch. IIIB <efg-ch-03b-metrical-en>`).
     - Threefold relational hierarchy: **Projective** (closed line, :person:`Pieri <pieri>`, :ref:`Ch. XLV <pom-ch-45-projective-en>`), **Descriptive** (open serial :term:`betweenness <betweenness>`, :person:`Pasch <pasch>`–:person:`Peano <peano>`, :ref:`Ch. XLVI <pom-ch-46-descriptive-en>`), and **Metrical** (:ref:`Ch. XLVII <pom-ch-47-metrical-en>`).
   * - **Von Staudt's Harmonic Range**
     - Admired (:ref:`EFG §§ 111–114 <efg-113>`), yet accused of a philosophical circle in defining points via lines and lines via points (:ref:`EFG §§ 115–119 <efg-115>`).
     - Celebrated as logically rigorous once points are taken as unanalyzable terms of Pierian incidence/harmonic relations; coordinates assigned via :term:`Möbius nets <moebius-net>` (:ref:`PoM §§ 365–370 <pom-365>`).
   * - **Motion & Congruence**
     - Ideal :term:`Free Mobility <free-mobility>` deduced *a priori* as homogeneity of the form of externality (:ref:`EFG §§ 146–154 <efg-147>`).
     - Literal motion of spatial points rejected as self-contradictory ("leopard changing his spots"); geometric :term:`motion <motion-geometric>` defined statically as a group of metric-preserving one-one point transformations (:ref:`PoM § 390 <pom-390>`).
   * - **Cayley–Klein Projective Metric**
     - Dismissed as a mere technical re-expression presupposing genuine metrical distance (:ref:`EFG §§ 37–42 <efg-37>`).
     - Vindicated logically: projective distance is a bona fide asymmetrical relation parameterized by an :term:`Absolute quadric <the-absolute>` (:ref:`PoM Ch. XLVIII, §§ 405–411 <pom-ch-48-relation-en>`).
   * - **Status of Spatial Points**
     - Infected with an inescapable Hegelian antinomy of the unextended spatial element (:ref:`EFG §§ 116–117 <efg-116>`, :ref:`§§ 189–204 <efg-198>`).
     - Logically consistent, unextended substantive terms; :person:`Cantor <cantor>`'s continuum $2^{\alpha_0}$ refutes :person:`Zeno <zeno>` and :person:`Lotze <lotze>` (:ref:`PoM Chs. L–LI, §§ 416–431 <pom-ch-50-continuity-en>`).
   * - **Kant's Spatial Intuition**
     - Reinterpreted sympathetically on its epistemological side while pruned of Euclidean narrowness (:ref:`EFG §§ 52–58 <efg-52>`).
     - Refuted root and branch: asymmetric relational logic generates serial order, dimensions, and chirality without subjective intuition (:ref:`PoM Ch. LII, §§ 432–436 <pom-ch-52-kant-en>`).

Why Both Treatises Remain Essential Together
--------------------------------------------

In the **GEOMETOR** corpus, the 1897 and 1903 treatises illuminate each other continuously:

1. **Historical Depth of Metageometry**: Chapters I and II of the 1897 *Essay* (:ref:`§§ 10–101 <efg-ch-01-metageometry-en>`) contain Russell's fullest historical and philosophical exposition of :person:`Gauss <gauss>`, :person:`Lobachevsky <lobatchewsky>`, :person:`Riemann <riemann>`, :person:`Helmholtz <helmholtz>`, :person:`Beltrami <beltrami>`, :person:`Cayley <cayley>`, :person:`Klein <klein>`, :person:`Lie <lie>`, :person:`Lotze <lotze>`, and :person:`Delboeuf <delboeuf>`—material presupposed rather than repeated in 1903.
2. **Visual & Projective Constructions**: The 1897 *Essay* preserves Russell's nine geometric diagrams (:ref:`Figs. 1–9 <efg-index>`), including spherical antipodal duality, hyperbolic limiting parallels, :person:`von Staudt <staudt>`'s complete quadrilateral construction (:ref:`Fig. 8, § 113 <efg-113>`), and the non-existence of a purely planar proof of :person:`Desargues <desargues>`'s theorem (:ref:`Fig. 9, Footnote 144 <efg-ch-03a-projective-en>`).
3. **Genesis of the Critique of Superposition**: Russell's famous 1903 polemic against :person:`Euclid <euclid>` ``I.4`` (:ref:`PoM §§ 390–391 <pom-390>`) directly sharpens the anti-empiricist argument against :person:`Helmholtz <helmholtz>` first worked out in :ref:`EFG §§ 70–73 <efg-70>` and :ref:`§§ 149–150 <efg-149>`, while purging it of the idealist "form of externality."
