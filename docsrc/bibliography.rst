.. _bibliography:

Bibliography & Cited Sources
============================

.. rubric:: Primary Russell Editions, 19th-Century Geometrical Sources, and GEOMETOR Critical Apparatus

This bibliography catalogues **24 foundational treatises, memoirs, and critical editions** referenced across the 1897 and 1903 Book Codexes and cross-corpus notes. Every inline ``:cite:`` role links directly to its corresponding entry below.

----

.. _cite-russell-1897:

Bertrand A. W. Russell — An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry (1897)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``russell:1897``
* **Publication / Locus:** Cambridge: At the University Press
* **Context:** First edition (xvi + 201 pp.); revised expansion of Russell's 1895 Trinity Fellowship dissertation. Public domain (Project Gutenberg #52091).

.. _cite-russell-1903:

Bertrand Russell — The Principles of Mathematics, Vol. I (1903)
---------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``russell:1903``
* **Publication / Locus:** Cambridge: At the University Press
* **Context:** First edition (xxix + 534 pp.), containing Part I (The Indefinables of Mathematics) and Part VI (Space, Chs. XLIV–LII, §§351–436, pp. 371–461).

.. _cite-russell-1899-axioms:

Bertrand Russell — Sur les axiomes de la géométrie (1899)
---------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``russell:1899:axioms``
* **Publication / Locus:** Revue de Métaphysique et de Morale 7, pp. 684–707
* **Context:** Russell's French exchange with Henri Poincaré marking his transition from the 1897 Essay toward Peano–Pieri axiomatics.

.. _cite-russell-1902-britannica:

Bertrand Russell — Geometry, Non-Euclidean (1902)
-------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``russell:1902:britannica``
* **Publication / Locus:** Encyclopaedia Britannica, 10th ed., Vol. XXVIII (Vol. IV of New Volumes), pp. 664–674
* **Context:** Cited by T. L. Heath in Euclid's Elements Vol. I, p. 227 on motion in geometry as transference of attention.

.. _cite-russell-1911-rmm:

Bertrand Russell — Le réalisme analytique (1911)
------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``russell:1911:rmm``
* **Publication / Locus:** Bulletin de la Société française de Philosophie / Revue de Métaphysique et de Morale
* **Context:** Cited by P. Hoenen in De Noetica Geometriae Caput III, §2.6.B on Cantorian point-set continua.

.. _cite-whitehead-russell-1910:

Alfred North Whitehead & Bertrand Russell — Principia Mathematica, Vol. I (1910)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``whitehead-russell:1910``
* **Publication / Locus:** Cambridge: At the University Press (``*2.01``, p. 100)
* **Context:** Formal symbolic execution of the logicist program announced in PoM (1903); analyzed by Hoenen in Caput V, §4.2.B.

.. _cite-hoenen-1954:

Petrus H. J. Hoenen, S.J. — De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis (1954)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``hoenen:1954``
* **Publication / Locus:** Analecta Gregoriana Vol. LXIII, Rome: Pontificia Universitas Gregoriana
* **Context:** Thomistic epistemological treatise engaging Russell's PoM §§389–391 in Caput IV (§§2–3, §7) and Russell's continuum/logicism in Caput III & V.

.. _cite-euclid-elements-i-1:

Euclid (trans. Sir Thomas L. Heath) — The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements (1908)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``euclid:elements:i-1``
* **Publication / Locus:** Vol. I, Book I, Prop. 1 (pp. 241–243)
* **Context:** Equilateral triangle construction critiqued in PoM §389 for assuming two-circle intersection without an explicit continuity postulate.

.. _cite-euclid-elements-i-4:

Euclid (trans. Sir Thomas L. Heath) — The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements (1908)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``euclid:elements:i-4``
* **Publication / Locus:** Vol. I, Book I, Prop. 4 & C.N. 4 (pp. 224–228, 247–250)
* **Context:** Side-Angle-Side congruence by superposition; locus of Russell's PoM §§390–391 critique, Heath's p. 249 commentary, and Hoenen's Caput IV §§2–3 defense.

.. _cite-pappus-collection-vii:

Pappus of Alexandria — Synagoge (Collection), Book VII (c. 340 AD)
------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``pappus:collection:vii``
* **Publication / Locus:** Props. 129–145 (Cross-Ratio & Harmonic Fourth)
* **Context:** Ancient projective lemmas underlying Desargues, Chasles, von Staudt, and Russell's projective chapters (geometor/pappus).

.. _cite-kant-1787-krv:

Immanuel Kant — Kritik der reinen Vernunft (Critique of Pure Reason, 2nd ed.) (1787)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``kant:1787:krv``
* **Publication / Locus:** Transcendental Aesthetic (B37–B49) & Transcendental Methodology (B740–B766)
* **Context:** Target of Russell's critical reconstruction in EFG Ch. II (§§51–58) and logicist refutation in PoM Ch. LII (§§432–436).

.. _cite-staudt-1847:

Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — Geometrie der Lage (and Beiträge zur Geometrie der Lage, 1856–1860) (1847)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``staudt:1847``
* **Publication / Locus:** Nürnberg: Friedrich Korn
* **Context:** Purely synthetic foundation of harmonic conjugates via the complete quadrilateral construction without metrical cross-ratios.

.. _cite-cayley-1859:

Arthur Cayley — A Sixth Memoir upon Quantics (1859)
---------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``cayley:1859``
* **Publication / Locus:** Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London 149, pp. 61–90
* **Context:** Introduced the projective theory of distance relative to the Absolute conic/quadric.

.. _cite-klein-1871:

Felix Klein — Über die sogenannte Nicht-Euklidische Geometrie (and Vorlesungen über Nicht-Euklidische Geometrie, 1893) (1871)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``klein:1871``
* **Publication / Locus:** Mathematische Annalen 4 (pp. 573–625) & 6 (pp. 112–145)
* **Context:** Unified hyperbolic, elliptic, and parabolic geometries under Cayley's projective metric.

.. _cite-riemann-1854:

Bernhard Riemann — Über die Hypothesen, welche der Geometrie zu Grunde liegen (1854 (pub. 1867))
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``riemann:1854``
* **Publication / Locus:** Abhandlungen der Königlichen Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen 13
* **Context:** Introduced n-dimensional manifolds, the quadratic differential metric ds², and spaces of constant curvature.

.. _cite-helmholtz-1868:

Hermann von Helmholtz — Über die Thatsachen, die der Geometrie zum Grunde liegen (1868)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``helmholtz:1868``
* **Publication / Locus:** Nachrichten von der Kgl. Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen, pp. 193–221
* **Context:** Formulated the free-mobility and rigid-body derivation of constant-curvature metrics.

.. _cite-pasch-1882:

Moritz Pasch — Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie (1882)
-------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``pasch:1882``
* **Publication / Locus:** Leipzig: B. G. Teubner
* **Context:** Axiomatized descriptive geometry, linear betweenness, planar separation (Pasch's axiom), and ideal elements.

.. _cite-peano-1889:

Giuseppe Peano — I principii di geometria logicamente esposti (and Sui fondamenti della geometria, 1894) (1889)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``peano:1889``
* **Publication / Locus:** Turin: Fratelli Bocca / Rivista di Matematica 4, pp. 51–90
* **Context:** Symbolic axiomatization of descriptive and metrical geometry studied in PoM Chs. XLVI–XLVII.

.. _cite-pieri-1898:

Mario Pieri — I principii della geometria di posizione composti in sistema logico deduttivo (1898)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``pieri:1898``
* **Publication / Locus:** Memorie della Reale Accademia delle Scienze di Torino (2) 48, pp. 1–62
* **Context:** Hypothetico-deductive axiomatization of projective geometry adopted by Russell throughout PoM Ch. XLV.

.. _cite-hilbert-1899:

David Hilbert — Grundlagen der Geometrie (1899)
-----------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``hilbert:1899``
* **Publication / Locus:** Festschrift zur Feier der Enthüllung des Gauss-Weber-Denkmals in Göttingen, Leipzig: Teubner
* **Context:** Modern axiomatic reconstruction of Euclidean geometry cited by Russell (PoM §§375, 390–391), Heath, and Hoenen.

.. _cite-lotze-1879:

Rudolf Hermann Lotze — Metaphysik: Drei Bücher der Ontologie, Kosmologie und Psychologie (1879)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``lotze:1879``
* **Publication / Locus:** Leipzig: S. Hirzel (Book II, Ch. II, §§108–137)
* **Context:** Source of the anti-metageometrical and anti-relational arguments examined in EFG §§85–96 and PoM §§424–428.

.. _cite-erdmann-1877:

Benno Erdmann — Die Axiome der Geometrie: Eine philosophische Untersuchung der Riemann-Helmholtz'schen Raumtheorie (1877)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``erdmann:1877``
* **Publication / Locus:** Leipzig: Johann Ambrosius Barth
* **Context:** Empiricist treatise on Riemann–Helmholtz geometry critiqued in EFG §§74–84.

.. _cite-whitehead-1898:

Alfred North Whitehead — A Treatise on Universal Algebra, with Applications, Vol. I (1898)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``whitehead:1898``
* **Publication / Locus:** Cambridge: At the University Press
* **Context:** Source for Russell's discussion of complex numbers, Grassmann's Ausdehnungslehre, and projective metrics in PoM Chs. XLIV & XLVIII.

.. _cite-hardy-1929:

G. H. Hardy — Mathematical Proof (1929)
---------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``hardy:1929``
* **Publication / Locus:** Mind 38 (149), pp. 1–25
* **Context:** Cited by Hoenen (Caput V, §3.2.A n. 8) regarding Russell's logistic proof of 2 + 2 = 4 vs. mathematical intuition.
