Historical & Cross-Corpus Commentary

Complete Reversal of 1897 EFG Chapter IV (§§196–208)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In :ref:`1897 Ch. IV <efg-198>`, Russell held that empty space is a 'bare possibility of relations' leading to the antinomy of the point unless anchored in unextended material atoms; in 1903 (§428 n.), he explicitly repudiates that Hegelian antinomy.
