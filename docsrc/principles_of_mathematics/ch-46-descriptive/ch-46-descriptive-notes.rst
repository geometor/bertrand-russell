Relation to Hilbert's Axiom Group II (1899)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Russell compares :person:`Peano <peano>`'s axioms with :person:`David Hilbert <hilbert>`'s :cite:`*Grundlagen der Geometrie* <hilbert:1899>` Group II (*Axiome der Anordnung*), noting that Peano's symbolic rigor anticipated Hilbert's planar separation postulates by a decade.
