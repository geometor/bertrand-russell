THE PRINCIPLES OF MATHEMATICS
=============================

.. rubric:: Vol. I — 1903 First Edition: Preface, Part I (Chapter I), and Part VI (*Space*, Chapters XLIV–LII)

.. container:: title-page

   | **THE PRINCIPLES OF MATHEMATICS**

   | CAMBRIDGE: AT THE UNIVERSITY PRESS — 1903

Editorial Scope & Public-Domain Provenance
------------------------------------------

This Book Codex presents the **unabridged, verbatim 1903 Cambridge University Press first edition** of the foundational and geometrical portions of :cite:`*The Principles of Mathematics* <russell:1903>`: the **1903 Preface** (pp. v–ix), **Part I, Chapter I** (*Definition of Pure Mathematics*, §§ 1–10, pp. 3–9), and the complete **Part VI: Space** (**Chapters XLIV–LII**, §§ 351–436, pp. 371–461)—comprising **96 numbered sections** in full, including **Chapter XLVII (§§ 389–391, pp. 404–407)** where Russell formulates his celebrated critique of :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s circle-intersection assumption (``I.1``) and method of :term:`superposition <superposition>` (``I.4``) directly answered by :person:`Fr. Petrus Hoenen, S.J. <hoenen>` in :cite:`*De Noetica Geometriae* <hoenen:1954>` (Caput IV) and cited by :person:`Sir Thomas L. Heath <heath>` in :cite:`*The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements* <euclid:elements:i-4>` (Vol. I, pp. 225–227, 249).

.. note::

   Russell's later Introduction to the 1937 George Allen & Unwin second edition remains under copyright    in several jurisdictions and is strictly **excluded** from this edition.
