Historical & Cross-Corpus Commentary
------------------------------------

Exclusion of the 1937 Second-Edition Introduction
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

This codex reproduces strictly the public-domain 1903 Cambridge University Press first edition (pp. v–ix), omitting Russell's later 1937 George Allen & Unwin retrospective introduction.

Contrast with the 1897 Preface
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Compare :ref:`1897 Preface <efg-preface-en>`, where Russell acknowledged obligations to F. H. Bradley, Sigwart, and Bosanquet; by December 1902, Bradleyan monism is replaced by :person:`Moore <moore>`'s pluralism and external relations.
