Historical & Cross-Corpus Commentary

Dialogue with Hoenen (Caput V: On Axiomatics)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In :cite:`*De Noetica Geometriae* <hoenen:1954>` (Caput V, §3.2.A n. 8 & §4.2.B), :person:`Fr. Peter Hoenen, S.J. <hoenen>` confronts Russell's §4 claim directly. Citing :person:`G. H. Hardy <hardy>` (:cite:`*Mind* 1929 <hardy:1929>`) and dissecting :cite:`*Principia Mathematica* <whitehead-russell:1910>` ``*2.01``, Hoenen argues that substituting tokens into a :term:`propositional function <propositional-function>` already presupposes spatial intuition of linear symbol order and equiformity.

Leibniz's Anticipation (§5)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Russell notes that :person:`Leibniz <leibniz>` sought to prove Euclidean axioms logically, failing only because he lacked asymmetric relational logic and assumed Euclidean space categorically necessary.
