=======
mission
=======

``geometor.bertrand_russell`` establishes a verified, public-domain digital critical edition of **Bertrand Russell's** two foundational Cambridge treatises on geometry—*An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry* (1897) and *The Principles of Mathematics* (1903, First Edition: Preface, Part I Ch. I, and Part VI *Space*).

Within the **GEOMETOR** ecosystem, Russell's treatises serve as the primary logicist and metageometrical interlocutor for three sister projects:

1. **Epistemology of Geometry (`geometor/hoenen`)**: Providing the exact 1903 pages (pp. 404–407, §§ 389–391) and surrounding relational continuum chapters answered point-by-point by :person:`Fr. Petrus Hoenen, S.J. <hoenen>` in *De Noetica Geometriae* (1954), Caput III–V.
2. **Classical Euclidean Foundations (`geometor/euclid` & `geometor/elements`)**: Supplying the full context for :person:`Sir Thomas L. Heath <heath>`'s engagement with Russell's critique of circle intersection in ``I.1``, triangle :term:`superposition <superposition>` in ``I.4``, and implicit order axioms in ``I.6``, ``I.7``, and ``I.16``.
3. **Synthetic Projective Geometry (`geometor/pappus`)**: Tracing the modern axiomatization of :person:`Pappus of Alexandria <pappus>`'s cross-ratio invariance and :person:`Karl Georg Christian von Staudt <staudt>`'s :term:`quadrilateral construction <quadrilateral-construction>` from Russell's 1897 circularity critique to his 1903 :person:`Pieri <pieri>`-inspired deductive synthesis.
