Editorial Scope & Diagram Reconstructions
-----------------------------------------

This Book Codex reproduces the **complete, unabridged 1897 Cambridge University Press first edition** of :cite:`*An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry* <russell:1897>` (xvi + 201 pp., **all 209 numbered sections §§ 1–209** and **all 171 authorial footnotes**), accompanied by **9 exact analytical vector SVG diagrams** newly reconstructed from Russell's geometric specifications—including :person:`von Staudt <staudt>`'s :term:`complete quadrilateral harmonic construction <quadrilateral-construction>` (Fig. 8, p. 125, :ref:`§ 113 <efg-113>`).
