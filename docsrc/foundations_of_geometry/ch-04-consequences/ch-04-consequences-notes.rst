Historical & Cross-Corpus Commentary

How Cantor and Moore Dissolved Chapter IV by 1903
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Chapter IV represents the high-water mark of Russell's neo-Hegelian dialectical phase (explicitly comparing empty space to Aristotle's *materia prima* in §203 and citing Hegel's *Naturphilosophie*). Within three years, Cantor's set-theoretic continuum (:ref:`PoM Ch. L <pom-422>`) and Moore's realism about absolute points (:ref:`PoM Ch. LI <pom-428>`) led Russell to repudiate all three antinomies as artifacts of faulty pre-relational logic.
