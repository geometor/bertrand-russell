Anticipation of PoM §390 and Convergence with Hoenen
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Russell's §§70–73 distinction between 'abstract geometrical matter' and physical rigid bodies directly anticipates :ref:`PoM §390 <pom-390>` and closely parallels—before Russell's 1903 turn against motion—:person:`Hoenen <hoenen>`'s :term:`materia intelligibilis <materia-intelligibilis>`!
