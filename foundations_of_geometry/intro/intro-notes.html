From 1897 Transcendental Apriority to 1903 Logicist Apriority¶

Compare EFG §5 with PoM §10 (1903): in 1897 apriority means ‘presupposed in possible sensory experience of externality’; in 1903 apriority means ‘derivable from pure logical constants alone’.
