Anticipation of PoM §390 and Convergence with Hoenen¶

Russell’s §§70–73 distinction between ‘abstract geometrical matter’ and physical rigid bodies directly anticipates PoM §390 and closely parallels—before Russell’s 1903 turn against motion—Hoenen’s materia intelligibilis!
