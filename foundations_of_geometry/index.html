AN ESSAY ON THE FOUNDATIONS OF GEOMETRY
BY BERTRAND A. W. RUSSELL, M.A.
FELLOW OF TRINITY COLLEGE, CAMBRIDGE
CAMBRIDGE: AT THE UNIVERSITY PRESS — 1897
To John McTaggart Ellis McTaggart, to whose discourse and friendship is owing the existence of this book.

Editorial Scope & Diagram Reconstructions¶

This Book Codex reproduces the complete, unabridged 1897 Cambridge University Press first edition of An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry (xvi + 201 pp., all 209 numbered sections §§ 1–209 and all 171 authorial footnotes), accompanied by 9 exact analytical vector SVG diagrams newly reconstructed from Russell’s geometric specifications—including von Staudt’s complete quadrilateral harmonic construction (Fig. 8, p. 125, § 113).
