Initial Dual Book Codex of Bertrand Russell’s Foundational Treatises on Geometry (1897 & 1903)¶

Compiled the complete multi-track Book Codex edition of Bertrand Russell’s two foundational Cambridge University Press treatises on geometry—An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry (1897) and The Principles of Mathematics (1903, 1st ed.)—together with a bidirectional cross-corpus concordance to geometor/hoenen, geometor/euclid, and geometor/pappus.

Highlights¶

  • Codex I — The Principles of Mathematics (1903, 1st Edition): Archived and compiled all 96 numbered sections across 11 3-track chapter units (English · Summary · Notes): the 1903 Preface, Part I Chapter I (Definition of Pure Mathematics, §§ 1–10), and all nine chapters of Part VI (Space, Chapters XLIV–LII, §§ 351–436, pp. 371–461), preserving original Cambridge pagination anchors (.. _pom-p404: through .. _pom-p407:) and strictly excluding the copyrighted 1937 second-edition introduction.

  • Codex II — An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry (1897): Compiled the unabridged treatise across 7 3-track chapter units covering all 209 numbered sections (§§ 1–209), all 171 author’s footnotes, and 9 analytical SVG geometric figures reconstructed in geometor.bertrand_russell.diagrams (including von Staudt’s quadrilateral construction of the harmonic fourth and Footnote 144’s planar Desargues failure diagram).

  • Cross-Corpus Scholarly Apparatus: Established SHA-256 verified primary source manifests, 10 detailed cross-corpus concordance loci (Master Concordance), 24 bibliographic entries (Bibliography), 38 prosopographical profiles (Index of Persons), and 25 technical glossary definitions (Glossary).
